A study companion for learning to understand and speak English by "deep listening": one lesson for many days, listening again and again, answering mini-story questions out loud.

A DAY-BY-DAY PLAN
• Steps follow the course guide: read → vocabulary → mini-story → point of view → main audio → commentary (optional).
• Each step has a listen target; reach it and the step ticks itself.
• Each lesson runs for N days (7 by default), a daily minutes goal, study streak and 14-day stats.

READ TO UNDERSTAND
• A text-only reading page to read at ease before listening.
• Tap a word to hear where the teacher explains it in the vocabulary lesson and mini-story, or open Google Translate.
• A Vietnamese meaning for each paragraph (when your material has one), hidden until you need it.

A PLAYER BUILT FOR LEARNERS
• Karaoke transcript: the playing sentence is highlighted and followed; tap a sentence to hear it again.
• Answer mode: pauses after each mini-story question so you can answer out loud.
• Shadowing: pauses after every sentence so you can repeat it.
• Repeats the track when it ends (or stops, or goes on); A–B loop, skip back/forward, speed 0.5–1.5x.
• Background playback with lock-screen and headset controls.
• Android Auto: study in the car with skip, speed and voice commands.

VOCABULARY & REVIEW
• Long-press a sentence to save a word or phrase as a card with the example sentence and its original audio.
• Spaced-repetition review (SM-2): Again / Hard / Good / Easy.

REMINDERS & LOOK
• Reminders at the times you choose, plus an evening nudge if you have not studied yet.
• Light, dark or system theme.

YOUR OWN MATERIAL
The app ships no course content. Use your own study material: connect Google Drive (read-only, a single folder you name) or pick a folder on the device. The app has no server and collects no data.
