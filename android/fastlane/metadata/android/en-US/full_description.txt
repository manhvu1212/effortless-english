A study companion for learning to understand and speak English by "deep listening": the same lesson for many days, answering mini-story questions out loud, shadowing sentence by sentence.

A DAY-BY-DAY PLAN
• Each lesson runs for N days (7 by default): read the transcript → main story → vocabulary → mini-story → point of view.
• Tick off each step; move on only when you understand nearly everything.
• Study streak, minutes listened per day, 14-day stats.

A PLAYER BUILT FOR LEARNERS
• Karaoke transcript: the playing sentence is highlighted and followed; tap a sentence to hear it again.
• Shadowing: pauses after every sentence so you can repeat it.
• A–B loop, sentence loop, skip back/forward, speed 0.5–1.5x.
• Background playback with lock-screen and headset controls.
• Android Auto: study in the car with skip, speed and voice commands.

VOCABULARY & REVIEW
• Long-press a sentence, pick a word or phrase and save it as a card with the example sentence and its original audio.
• Spaced-repetition review (SM-2): Again / Hard / Good / Easy.

STUDY REMINDERS
• Reminders at the times you choose, plus an evening nudge if you have not studied yet.

YOUR OWN MATERIAL
The app ships no course content. Use your own study material: connect Google Drive (read-only, a single folder you name) or pick a folder on the device. The app has no server and collects no data.
